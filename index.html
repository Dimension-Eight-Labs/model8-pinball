HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Flippers
Hold the left or right half of the screen. Hold to cradle the ball, let go to drop it.
Launch
Drag down on the right side while the ball is in the BOOT lane, then let go. Further pull, harder shot. A tap gives a standard shot.
Nudge
Bump the phone. A sideways bump pushes the ball the other way; a forward bump lifts it. iOS asks to allow motion when you tap BOOT. Turn it off with BUMP NUDGE on the start screen.
Tilt
Four nudges in a few seconds is TILT: the flippers go dead and you lose that ball’s bonus. DANGER is your warning.

Scoring

L-O-A-D lanes
Light all four to raise the bonus multiplier: 2×, 4×, 8×, 16×. Flipping shifts the lit lanes.
Punch card
Knock down all five drop targets: 10,000 and 2 core bits.
Bus ramp
2,500 and 1 core bit.
Core memory
Sink the saucer: 1,000 and 1 core bit. Eight bits make a byte: 50,000.
Tubes & tape
Bumpers 100, slingshots 10, tape spinner 25 a spin.
Balls
Three balls. A ball that drains in the first 8 seconds is saved. Each ball ends with its bonus times the multiplier.

Keyboard

Z / ← left flipper, / / → right flipper, hold Space to pull and release to launch, N nudges, P pauses.
